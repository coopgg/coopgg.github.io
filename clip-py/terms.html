What Clip.py does

Clip.py runs on your own computer. It reads video files you give it (your own recordings, or your own past broadcasts that it downloads from Twitch), finds highlights, and makes video files on your computer. If you connect your TikTok account, it can upload the videos it made to your TikTok inbox as drafts. It never publishes anything on its own: you decide what to post, and you post it from TikTok.
