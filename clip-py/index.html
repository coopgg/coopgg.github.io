Clip.py

Your stream's best moments, clipped automatically.

Clip.py is a free desktop highlight tool that automatically reviews gaming streams and recordings, finds the best moments with computer vision, and turns them into music-synced highlight montages.

  • Finds every playIt reads the in-game kill feed, checks each kill on screen, and cuts the best ones into clips.
  • Builds the montageClips are cut to the beat of your music, building up to the best moment, with an end card.
  • Made for verticalEvery montage also comes as a 9:16 video, with your webcam on top when it's on.
  • You post itClip.py can send finished videos to your own TikTok inbox as drafts. You review, caption and post each one yourself.
  • Runs on your PCEverything happens on your own computer. Clip.py has no servers and no accounts.
