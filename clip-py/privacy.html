Clip.py

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version: Clip.py runs entirely on your own computer. CoopGG doesn't run any servers for it, doesn't collect your data, and never sees your videos or your accounts.

What Clip.py works with

  • Your videos. Clip.py reads the recordings and stream videos you give it, and saves the clips and montages it makes, in folders on your computer. They are not sent anywhere unless you choose to upload them to TikTok.
  • Your Twitch past broadcasts. If you set a Twitch channel, Clip.py downloads that channel's public past broadcasts to your computer. It does not sign in to Twitch.
  • Your webcam image in videos. To build vertical videos, Clip.py looks for a face in the video frames to find where the webcam is. This happens on your computer, only to decide the layout. No face data is stored or sent anywhere.

TikTok

If you connect your TikTok account, you sign in on TikTok's own page and approve the access Clip.py asks for:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Uploading videos (video.upload), used only to send the videos Clip.py made on your computer to your own TikTok inbox as drafts. Clip.py does not post publicly, and does not read your videos, followers, messages or other TikTok data.

The login that TikTok gives Clip.py (an access token) is saved only on your computer, in Clip.py's folder. It is sent only to TikTok, when uploading. To disconnect, remove Clip.py from the connected apps in your TikTok account settings, and delete the saved login file. Your use of TikTok is also covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Nothing. Clip.py has no analytics, tracking, ads or accounts, and doesn't send any information to CoopGG. We don't sell or share data, because we never have it.

Other services

Clip.py connects only to the services you set up: Twitch (to download your past broadcasts) and TikTok (to upload your drafts). Each has its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of it.

Children

Clip.py is not directed at children under 13, and anyone using it with TikTok must meet TikTok's own age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
